🤖 AI-Powered Workflow

Automated Vision Pipeline: When photos are attached, the Python FastAPI AI service categorizes severity and routes the ticket directly to the corresponding department.
  • 1. Citizen Reports: Ticket is assigned an instant unique tracking ID.
  • 2. AI Classification: Deep learning model classifies road, water, electricity, or sanitation hazards.
  • 3. Department Routing: Auto-dispatched to the respective municipal division.
  • 4. Field Resolution: On-site officers update repair status with public resolution logs.
